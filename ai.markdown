This policy covers my views and use of LLMs and similar generative output
automations often marketted as "Artificial Intelligence" or "AI." It does not
(generally) include the broader, non-generative world of machine learning at
large.

As with all opinions, this one is soley my own and it is subject to change. It
is nuanced rather than strictly black and white. It is meant not to be used as
a weapon against others but to serve as a basis for better understanding my
experience.

{: .callout}
**TL;DR** - This site, it's content and construction are entirely **No-AI**.
<br>
Only a human touches these files, tends to the content and performs the
maintenance for this site. I do not, and *will not* use AI for this space.
<br>
<br>
In my professional career I may occasionally use AI if it is provided by my
work and is allowed. I limit its use and only wield it as a limited tool in an
otherwise vast and predominately human centric toolbox. I do not use AI/LLMs to
make decisons or to "think" on my behalf, and I verify its outputs with my own
two eyes and brain-cells.
